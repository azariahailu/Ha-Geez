# ሀ ግእዝ

A free community dictionary from **Ge'ez to Amharic**.

Open it here: https://hageezlexicon.vercel.app

Ge'ez is the liturgical language of the Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox churches. ሀ ግእዝ gathers more than 13,000 Ge'ez words, each with its origin and its meaning in Amharic, so anyone can look a word up in seconds.

## What you can do

* Search by a Ge'ez word or by its Amharic meaning
* Browse words by their Fidel letter family
* Type with or without spaces. `ቤተ ክርስቲያን` and `ቤተክርስቲያን` find the same word
* Read a short history of the Ge'ez language, its script, and its poetry (Qenie)
* Send a word you know, with its meaning, so it can be added to the dictionary
* Get in touch through the Contact page

## Adding a word

Know a word that's missing? Open **Submit**, add the Ge'ez word, where it comes from if you know, and its Amharic meaning. You can send several words at once. Each one is checked before it appears in the dictionary.

## Sources

The word list comes from books, published sources, and words sent in by readers. The history of Ge'ez on the About page draws on Isaias Haileab Gebrai, "Ge'ez," Mahibere Kidusan / EOTC Sunday School Department (2022), [eotcmk.org/e/geez](https://eotcmk.org/e/geez/).
